# Reflection

After completing your maze-solving robot, reflect on the engineering process.

## What Worked Well?
- Which design choices were effective?
- Which parts of your algorithm performed reliably?

## What Was Challenging?
- Sensor calibration?
- Wiring?
- Algorithm tuning?
- Mechanical stability?

## How Did You Debug Issues?
- What tests helped you identify problems?
- What changes improved performance?

## What Would You Improve?
- Sensor placement?
- Algorithm enhancements?
- Code modularity?
- Mechanical design?

## Engineering Mindset
Reflect on how you:
- Iterated your design
- Solved problems systematically
- Used testing to refine your robot
- Communicated your technical decisions
